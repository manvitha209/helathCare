# 👤 User Guide - How to Use MedPredict AI

**MedPredict AI** helps you assess your risk for Diabetes and Heart Disease using artificial intelligence.

> ⚠️ **Important**: This tool is for educational purposes. Always consult with your doctor about your health.

---

## 🎯 What is MedPredict AI?

MedPredict AI is a **risk assessment tool** that uses machine learning to analyze your health data and estimate your risk level for:
- **Diabetes** (Sugar regulation disorder)
- **Heart Disease** (Cardiovascular problems)

It's like a quick health check that helps you understand your risk level.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the App
Open your web browser and go to: **http://127.0.0.1:5000**

You'll see the MedPredict AI home page.

### Step 2: Choose a Disease to Check
Click one of these tabs:
- 💉 **Diabetes Predictor** - Check your diabetes risk
- ❤️ **Heart Disease Predictor** - Check your heart disease risk

### Step 3: Enter Your Health Information

Fill in your measurements. If you're not sure about a value:
- Check your recent medical records
- Ask your doctor
- Click "Try sample" to see examples

### Step 4: View Your Results

After submitting, you'll see:
- **Risk Score** (0-100%) - How high your risk is
- **Risk Level** - Category like "Low", "Moderate", or "High"
- **Color-coded gauge** - Visual representation
- **Personalized message** - Explanation of your results
- **Recommendations** - Tips to reduce your risk

### Step 5: Take Action

Share your results with your doctor and follow the recommendations.

## 📊 Understanding Your Results

### Risk Levels

| Level | Range | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| ✅ Very Low | 0-20% | Excellent - Keep up current habits |
| 🟢 Low | 20-40% | Good - Minor lifestyle adjustments |
| ⚠️ Moderate | 40-60% | Caution - More attention needed |
| 🔶 High | 60-80% | Alert - Lifestyle changes recommended |
| 🚨 Very High | 80-100% | Critical - Seek immediate medical advice |

### Example

**If your diabetes risk is 65%:**
- Your risk level is: **High**
- Color: 🔶 Orange
- Meaning: You have moderate-to-high risk of diabetes
- Action: Follow recommendations and see a doctor

## 💡 Tips for Using the App

### Fill Information Accurately
The better your data, the better the results. For each field:
- **Age**: Your current age in years
- **Blood Pressure**: Often shown as two numbers (120/80)
- **Glucose**: Your blood sugar level (from blood tests)
- **BMI**: Your Body Mass Index - use an online calculator if unsure
- Other measurements: From recent medical exams

### Use Sample Data to Practice
- Click **"✅ Healthy Profile"** to see an example low-risk profile
- Click **"⚠️ High Risk Profile"** to see high-risk example
- Try both to understand how values affect results

### Update Periodically
- Check your results every 3-6 months
- As your lifestyle changes, so might your risk
- Track improvements over time

## 📋 What You Need to Know

### Diabetes Assessment

**Tests these factors:**
- Pregnancies (women only)
- Blood glucose level
- Blood pressure
- Insulin levels
- Body weight (BMI)
- Family history (Pedigree Function)
- Age

**Time to complete**: 2-3 minutes

### Heart Disease Assessment

**Tests these factors:**
- Age and sex
- Chest pain type
- Blood pressure
- Cholesterol level
- Heart rate
- ECG results
- And more...

**Time to complete**: 3-4 minutes

## ⚠️ Important Information

### What This Tool CAN Do
✓ Estimate relative risk level  
✓ Motivate healthy lifestyle changes  
✓ Provide talking points with your doctor  
✓ Track changes over time  

### What This Tool CANNOT Do
✗ Diagnose disease  
✗ Replace medical tests  
✗ Provide medical advice  
✗ Predict exact outcomes  

### When to See a Doctor IMMEDIATELY
🚨 **Don't wait if you have:**
- Chest pain or pressure
- Severe shortness of breath
- Sudden dizziness or fainting
- Symptoms of stroke (slurred speech, weakness)
- Persistent high blood sugar symptoms

## 🏥 Sharing Results With Your Doctor

### Print Your Results
1. After seeing results, press **Ctrl+P** (or Cmd+P on Mac)
2. Choose "Print to PDF" or print directly
3. Include the date and risk score

### Discussion Points
- Ask your doctor: "Is this risk level accurate for me?"
- Ask: "What should I do to lower my risk?"
- Ask: "How often should I get these checks?"
- Ask: "Do I need additional tests?"

### Medical Records
- Screenshot or print your results
- Keep with other health documents
- Share during check-ups

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is this a medical diagnosis?
**A:** No. This is a risk assessment tool only. Always get proper diagnosis from a doctor.

### Q: Can this replace my doctor?
**A:** No. Use this as a conversation starter with your healthcare provider.

### Q: How accurate is this?
**A:** The models are 82-84% accurate on average. Individual accuracy varies. Your doctor's assessment is more reliable.

### Q: Can I use this for someone else?
**A:** Yes, but each person should enter their own data. Don't alter measurements.

### Q: How often should I check?
**A:** Every 3-6 months if you're managing risk, or as recommended by your doctor.

### Q: What if my results are scary?
**A:** First, remember this is an estimate. Second, it's motivating! High risk means you can take action to improve. See your doctor to discuss.

### Q: Do you store my information?
**A:** No. All data is processed instantly and not saved. Your privacy is protected.
