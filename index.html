About This Tool

Smart Support for Early Health Awareness

MedPredict AI uses trained Random Forest machine learning models to assess your risk for two of the most prevalent chronic conditions — Diabetes and Heart Disease. It also presents practical next-step care options so health awareness can lead to action.

  • No appointment or sign-up required — completely free
  • Plain-English results anyone can understand
  • Personalised recommendations included with every report
  • Your data is never stored or shared

Simple Process

How It Works

Three simple steps to get your personalised health risk report — no medical background needed.

01
Enter Your Measurements

Fill in basic clinical values like blood pressure, glucose and BMI. Every field includes a plain-English hint explaining what it is and where to find it on your lab report.

02
AI Analyses Your Data

Our Random Forest model — trained on thousands of clinical records — processes your inputs in milliseconds to calculate a personalised risk probability score.

03
Get Your Risk Report

Receive a clear risk percentage, a plain-English explanation of what it means, and a personalised list of next steps and lifestyle recommendations.
